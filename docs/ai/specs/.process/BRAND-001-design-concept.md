# BRAND-001 Design Concept

## Goals

- Define one coherent visual system with Racecraft as the parent, Racecraft
  Plugins as the repository/docs identity, and SpecKit Pro by Racecraft as the
  endorsed product identity.
- Speak first to hands-on founders, technical leads, and staff engineers who
  need to standardize serious agentic software delivery.
- Express the promise that Racecraft turns product intent into reviewable,
  reliable agent execution across Claude Code and Codex.
- Preserve useful Racecraft recognition while replacing weak or inconsistent
  geometry with a distinctive, scalable system.
- Produce four genuinely different original SVG concept families that can be
  compared fairly in real launch contexts.
- Make confident precision, small-size clarity, and maintainable vector
  construction more important than decorative complexity.
- Leave a rationale-separated comparison packet ready for independent critique
  and human selection in BRAND-002.

## Non-goals

- Selecting a winning family or refining canonical masters; those are owned by
  BRAND-002 and BRAND-003.
- Sanitization, optimization, raster export automation, or platform packaging.
- README, Starlight, marketplace, Claude Code, or Codex integration.
- Final trademark clearance or a claim that AI-produced artwork is legally clear.
- A technical reset of the existing SpecKit Pro version history to `1.0.0`.

## Design Tree and Decision Log

### Q1 — Identity continuity

**Question:** How much continuity should the new identity preserve from
Racecraft's current visual language?

**Selected:** Evolve it (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** Preserve recognizable cues such as Racecraft crimson,
precision, and controlled motion, but permit new core geometry. The current mark
is a legacy reference, not a shape that concepts must redraw.

### Q2 — Core metaphor

**Question:** What should the strongest visual idea behind the logo family be?

**Selected:** Precision system (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** Explore abstract apex, racing-line, gate, and
specification relationships. Do not lead with a literal car, helmet, steering
wheel, checkered flag, speed streak, or generic code bracket.

### Q3 — Color architecture

**Question:** How should Racecraft and SpecKit Pro relate through color?

**Selected:** Shared system, distinct accents (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** Use Racecraft crimson for the parent and SpecKit Pro
indigo for the product, joined by shared neutrals, construction logic, and
geometry. Every concept must also work in monochrome; color cannot be the only
evidence that the brands belong together.

### Q4 — Product endorsement

**Question:** When should SpecKit Pro visibly carry the Racecraft endorsement?

**Selected:** First touch required (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** Test `SpecKit Pro by Racecraft` on docs, README,
marketplace, and launch-style first-touch surfaces. A compact SpecKit Pro symbol
or lockup may stand alone in constrained plugin and small-icon contexts.

### Q5 — Wordmark strategy

**Question:** What level of custom typography should BRAND-001 pursue?

**Selected:** Existing type, custom details (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** Begin with the current self-hosted type system and
explore limited custom letterform or lockup details. The brief must record the
font source, license, and whether final wordmark geometry remains live type or
becomes outlined paths.

### Q6 — Visual tone

**Question:** Which quality should dominate when the marks are viewed at small size?

**Selected:** Confident precision (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** Favor simple silhouettes, strong negative space,
controlled proportions, and calm technical authority over aggressive slants,
glows, gradients, or ornamental speed effects.

### Q7 — Reviewable slicing

**Question:** The advisory estimator suggested three slices because the original
identity foundation counted up to 20 visual and review files; how should it be
structured?

**Selected:** Three separate specs

**Decision consequence:** BRAND-001 owns the brief and four comparable concept
families. BRAND-002 owns rationale-blind critique and human selection. BRAND-003
owns refinement and canonical SVG master production. The revised BRAND-001 size
signal is one user story, eight core authored files, and three functional
requirements; the advisory estimate is 390 with one suggested slice. The whole
branch must remain at or below 25 files after adding scaffold and generated
SpecKit artifacts.

### Q8 — Primary launch audience

**Question:** Who should the Racecraft identity brief treat as the primary
soft-launch audience?

**Selected:** Agentic engineering leaders (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** Design first for hands-on founders, technical leads,
and staff engineers who are standardizing serious Claude Code and Codex
workflows. Solo developers and platform organizations remain secondary audiences.

### Q9 — Product promise

**Question:** Which promise should anchor the Racecraft and SpecKit Pro identity?

**Selected:** Intent to reliable execution (Recommended)

**Decision consequence:** The brief and concept rationales must express the
promise: turn product intent into reviewable, reliable agent execution across
Claude Code and Codex. Speed and cross-agent consistency support that promise;
they do not replace it.

## Concept Territory Requirements

The author must produce four structurally different families, not four styling
variants of one silhouette. These territories are starting constraints rather
than preselected answers:

1. **Apex Gate** — A continuous trajectory creates or passes through an open
   gate, emphasizing decisive entry, constraint, and forward movement.
2. **Tuned Trajectory** — A controlled path, apex, or paired control points
   communicates iterative steering and precision without resembling a map pin.
3. **Modular Standard** — Constructed modules, frames, or aligned planes express
   specifications becoming an executable system without becoming a document icon.
4. **Shared Negative Space** — Parent and product forms derive from one negative-
   space rule, proving the endorsed family relationship without relying on color.

Each family must include:

- A parent Racecraft symbol direction.
- A Racecraft Plugins horizontal-lockup direction.
- A SpecKit Pro symbol and endorsed-lockup direction.
- Light, dark, and monochrome presentation.
- 16 px, 24 px, and 32 px symbol tests.
- README, docs-header, and plugin-list context previews using identical frames.
- A section in `brand/review/author-rationales.md` containing the private author
  rationale, provenance statement, and candid known weakness.

`brand/README.md` must define the neutral allowed-input manifest for BRAND-002,
hash the brief, four family SVGs, and comparison board, and explicitly exclude
the private rationale file. BRAND-002 must build its clean critic snapshot from
those recorded inputs.

## Evaluation Rubric

BRAND-001 must encode this rubric in `brand/brief.md` so BRAND-002 can apply it
without seeing the author rationales:

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Distinctive silhouette | 15% | Recognizable in monochrome and separated from familiar software/racing clichés |
| Small-size clarity | 20% | Survives 16 px, 24 px, and 32 px tests without accidental fills or noise |
| Brand architecture | 20% | Racecraft, Racecraft Plugins, and SpecKit Pro read as related but distinguishable |
| Concept fit | 15% | Expresses confident precision, guidance, and specification-driven execution |
| Balance and craft | 10% | Proportions, optical weight, spacing, and negative space remain intentional across lockups |
| Theme versatility | 10% | Works on light, dark, and single-color surfaces |
| Practical construction | 5% | Uses editable SVG geometry with a stable viewBox and no external dependency |
| Originality risk | 5% | Avoids obvious resemblance and identifies areas needing human knockout review |

## Open Questions

- Which exact legacy geometry cues, if any, survive the initial asset audit
  without weakening originality? The concept author must show the decision in
  the brief rather than silently copying the current mark.
- Which candidates require a human trademark knockout search before BRAND-002
  can select them?
- Which limited wordmark customizations remain readable and licensable after
  the four concept families are compared?

These questions are execution inputs, not blockers to scaffolding BRAND-001.
