# Contract: Replay And Smoke Evidence

## Deterministic Replay

The implementation publishes deterministic replay cases under:

```text
tests/speckit-pro/layer6-efficiency/fixtures-codex-controls/replay-cases.json
```

Replay is the automated evidence path. Every control has positive and seeded
negative cases. Running the same replay twice must produce byte-identical
governed results and zero outcome-bearing scored rows.

Replay covers:

- registry closed-at-three and duplicate-kind refusal
- digest recomputation and frozen-binding drift refusal
- unpinned parent-context identity and absent override checks
- adaptive total response maps, precedence, consistency, route movement,
  clean-pass accounting, bounds, and service-reroute non-scorability
- justified-high-effort eligibility and parent-plus-children aggregation
- comparison floors, dimensions, margins, zero denominator, confidence,
  multiplicity, and claim-class mapping
- reserved objective admission refusal
- twin category 7/8 executable behavior

## Smoke Planning

Live smoke execution is operator-only. The implementation may prepare a smoke
plan and sealer for the three selected observables, but must not execute live
work without explicit authorization.

An admissible smoke plan requires:

- `authentication_mode: chatgpt_subscription`
- no API-key override
- no reserved G56R-011 objective
- non-scored labeling
- all frozen objective, repetition, token, component, cache, and time bounds
- off-repository raw capture retention

## Smoke Sealing

A smoke record is sealed from produced evidence, never dispatch intent.

Required produced-evidence observables:

- Unpinned: served model and effort equal the pinned parent; local model,
  effort, provider, service-tier, and API-key overrides are absent.
- Adaptive: qualifying signal plus served route, model, and effort move from
  declared ladder index `i` to `i + 1`.
- Justified high effort: frozen route/model/effort, true eligibility result, and
  complete parent-plus-children aggregate over all children actually spawned.

Refusal conditions:

- observed `api_key`, missing auth observation, or ambiguous auth mode
- reserved objective consumption
- bound breach
- shared, unobserved, path-based, or missing cache-isolation root
- missing produced evidence for exact treatment
- committed raw prompt, model response, local path, or operator capture content

An unrun smoke remains `unrun`; it is never passed, waived, or inferred.
